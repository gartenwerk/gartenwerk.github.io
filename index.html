Garten: Cozy Farm & Bakery

Garten: Cozy Farm & Bakery is a cozy offline farm game for Android. Grandma has moved to the sea and left you her overgrown farm: grow crops, help the village baker, cook pickles and jam, and hire helpers who keep the farm running while you rest. It plays without internet, has no in-app purchases, and ads are only optional reward videos.

Coming soon to Google Play. The game is in closed testing right now.

What you do in Garten

  • Plant, water and harvest — from wheat to watermelons — and carry your goods to the stalls.
  • Hire gardeners and carriers: a relaxing idle farm that keeps earning even while the game is closed.
  • Help the baker in a match-3 mini-game, cook pickles and jam, find your way through foggy mazes.
  • Meet the neighbors — each has a story — and read new pages of Grandma's diary.
  • Short 5–10 minute sessions with daily goals. No fights, no rush.
